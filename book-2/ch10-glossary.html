Lowest common denominator (LCD)

The smallest number (or expression) that all the denominators divide into.

ExampleThe LCD of 2/3 and 1/5 is 15.

Equivalent fractions

Fractions that look different but have the same value.

Example2/3 = 10/15

Long division

A step-by-step way of dividing one expression by another, like long division with numbers.

Example(2x² + 7x + 3) ÷ (x + 3) = 2x + 1
